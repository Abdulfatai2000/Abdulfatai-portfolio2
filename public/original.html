CONCEPT PREVIEW

Dovia

PRODUCTIVITY

A meeting-to-action app designed to turn scattered notes into reviewed decisions, tasks, and follow-ups.

Behind the build ＋
Meeting-to-action productivity

Too many meetings. Too little follow-through.

I started with a simple question: what happens after a meeting ends? Dovia brings the next steps into one workspace, connecting meeting notes, reviewed action items, tasks, and follow-ups.

The design challenge

Keeping people in control of AI suggestions: an action item should be reviewed, assigned, and confirmed before it becomes work.

CONCEPT PREVIEW

AfyaPulse

HEALTHTECH

A team hackathon project connecting citizen health reporting with tools for health officers. My role: Citizen App Lead.

Behind the build ＋
Citizen App Lead · Team hackathon project

A citizen report can be the beginning of a bigger picture.

Working as Citizen App Lead meant focusing on the first interaction: helping people find their way through onboarding, reporting, health records, and alerts. The officer portal, backend, and security were shared responsibilities across the team.

What collaboration taught me

A good interface needs clear handoffs. Citizen workflows and officer workflows must connect through agreed data and permissions.

Somamap

HEALTHTECH

A body-map symptom tracking project with timelines and patterns. Built for the OAU Ontomorph Hackathon, where it placed 7th.

Behind the build ＋
Solo developer · 7th place

Symptoms tell a story over time.

Somamap explores symptom tracking through a body map, timelines, and patterns. Building it for the OAU Ontomorph Hackathon pushed me to learn the Ontomorph DTP SDK and HOLON API while turning the idea into a working web experience.

The milestone

The project placed 7th at the OAU Ontomorph Hackathon. It gave me experience integrating unfamiliar tools and explaining a product to judges.

CONCEPT PREVIEW

CampusCare

MOBILE

A mobile learning project exploring campus issue reporting, emergency contact display, and appointment navigation.

Behind the build ＋
Mobile learning prototype

Start small. Make every interaction count.

CampusCare began as a focused React Native assignment: show a campus service, respond to a button press, and navigate to an appointment screen. It is a learning prototype, with simulated reporting and a sample emergency contact.

The lesson

Small screens teach clarity. State, feedback, and navigation need to work together so a user always understands what happened.
